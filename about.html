About the Club

Why Join the 19 Wing Karate Club?

Our dojo combines deep martial arts discipline with a welcoming, fitness-focused community. Whether you are stepping onto the mats for the very first time or looking to sharpen advanced skills, our dedicated coaches support every level with clear goals and positive training.

What We Offer

  • Structured Progression: Clear, goal-oriented belt advancements under the traditional Goju Ryu system.
  • Community Training: A friendly, supportive group environment for all ages.
  • Events & Seminars: Regular tournaments, demonstrations, and special events across Vancouver Island.
  • Professional Instruction: High Five certified instructors holding comprehensive background checks.

Our National Lineage

The Comox Traditional Karate Club is part of the Meibukan Goju-Ryu family across Canada, under the senior guidance of Sensei Hing-Poon Chan. Sensei Chan oversees training standards nationally, including requesting permission for our students to train in Okinawa and coordinating joint training with our sister clubs across the country.

Sensei Hing-Poon Chan

Sensei Chan began studying Goju-Ryu karate in Hong Kong in March 1968, at age 14, under Mr. Kim-Hung Wong. Training three hours daily, he advanced quickly, earning his brown belt within the first year and his Shodan (1st degree black belt) in January 1971.

He immigrated to Canada in November 1971 and continued his training after settling in Ontario. In 1985, he founded the Gloucester Traditional Karate Association (GTKA), and in 1988 became affiliated with the Meibukan family. In 1994, he travelled to Okinawa and trained directly with Meitatsu and Meitetsu Yagi, sons of Dai Sensei Meitoku Yagi, founder of Meibukan Goju-Ryu.

Beyond karate, Sensei Chan has also studied traditional weapons forms (Bo, Sai, and Tonfa), as well as Wing Chun, Northern Shaolin, and Pa Kua Kung Fu, deepening the breadth of instruction he brings to the Meibukan family in Canada.
