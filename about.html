About the Club

class="page-text"

Why Join the 19 Wing Karate Club?

Our dojo combines deep martial arts discipline with a welcoming, fitness-focused community. Whether you are stepping onto the mats for the very first time or looking to sharpen advanced skills, our dedicated coaches support every level with clear goals and positive training.

What We Offer

  • Structured Progression: Clear, goal-oriented belt advancements.
  • Community Training: A friendly, supportive group environment for all ages.
  • Events & Seminars: Regular tournaments, demonstrations, and special events.

The Art of Okinawa Goju Ryu

Unlike modern sport-focused martial arts, the Comox Traditional Karate Club teaches classical Okinawa Goju Ryu Karate-Do. Known as the "hard and soft" style, our training prioritizes practical self-defense, situational awareness, and deep personal development.

A Safe, Professional Environment

Your safety and privacy are our top priorities. All instructors have passed comprehensive criminal background checks, and our teaching team is proudly High Five certified.

Note: As a physical activity, all members are required to sign a standard liability waiver and provide confidential health/contact information upon joining.
